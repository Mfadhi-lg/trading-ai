DI-03 · 2026-10-27 · Protocol `DI03-credit-labeling/1.0.0` · **REGRESSION ONLY**

This is an empty human annotation package: 12 originals, 60 document/field pairs, zero completed labels, zero independent submissions and zero adjudications. No model experiment has passed. The original files were opened read-only and their SHA256, byte sizes and PDF page counts matched the existing manifest when this package was prepared. This checks accessibility and byte identity; humans must still verify visual readability, legal entities and all claim evidence.

The frozen authority is `FEATURE_DICTIONARY_V1.md` in the parent directory, contract `document-intelligence-features/1.0.0-draft`, especially sections 3, 4A, 5, 7 and 8. These instructions apply its five specified fields without adding definitions or mappings. Unknown publication/capture history does not prevent source annotation, but does prevent historical ML admission until the separate dictionary gates pass.

## Start here — no programming required

1. A coordinator names reviewers A and B and a human adjudicator. Give each reviewer a private folder or access-controlled workspace containing this guide, the source list and separate copies of the coverage and claim templates. Keep the adjudication template with the coordinator until independent submissions are sealed. Do not use a shared workbook or reveal the other reviewer's progress or answers.
2. Provide access to the original PDFs at the existing locators. Relative locators start at the repository root, not this guide's folder. In Finder or a PDF viewer, navigate to that location and open the PDF. D18 needs the mounted `My Passport` drive. Do not download replacements or copy the large PDFs into this package. If access fails, tell the coordinator and record `DOCUMENT_UNAVAILABLE`; never substitute a similar report.
3. Open CSVs in Excel, LibreOffice or another spreadsheet editor using UTF-8 comma-separated import. Import **all columns as text**, preserving hashes, IDs, dates, minus signs and rating modifiers. Use text format before typing quotations that start with `=`, `+`, `-` or `@`; they are source text, not spreadsheet formulas. Keep the original templates blank. Name working copies, for example, `A_coverage_v1.csv` and `A_claims_v1.csv`. Retain headers when saving as UTF-8 CSV. Quote-containing or multiline cells should be saved by the spreadsheet's CSV export.
4. Read the whole original, visually, from first physical PDF page to last, for all five fields. Do not begin from suggested pages. Review cover letters, current and historical rating tables, annexures, financial summaries, headers, notes and continuations. Use the PDF viewer's search only as an aid; keyword search alone cannot establish absence.
5. For each field fill its coverage row and add as many claim rows as necessary. A blank cell means not recorded, never a negative answer. Record unresolved questions rather than inventing values or dates. Use the completion and state rules below.
6. Sign and submit both CSVs privately. The coordinator preserves their exact bytes, records full file SHA256 using an approved checksum utility, and seals them before comparison. Both submissions must be complete before either reviewer sees the other's labels. Hashing/sealing is a coordinator task; reviewers need no scripts.

**Contamination controls:** give reviewers only these five package files and the exact originals. Do not distribute DI-02 readiness tables/manifest, candidate page hints, candidate availability decisions, previous Qwen/Gemini responses, agent extractions, correction files or comparison results. The frozen dictionary contains historical links: distribute its normative definitions without opening historical evaluation links or summaries. Reviewers must independently discover evidence before any optional navigation hints; this workflow keeps hints withheld through submission. Record prior exposure honestly in every submission. A prior exposed reviewer cannot attest to being unexposed; the coordinator must disclose the limitation or reassign review. Record an independence statement confirming human source inspection, no AI assistance, no other-reviewer answers and any prior exposure. A checksum cannot prove independence.

## Five field definitions and review checks

**`cr_long_term_rating`** is the exact long-term agency grade for the named rated legal entity and instrument/facility. Preserve agency prefix, scale, plus/minus, enhancement, provisional, noncooperating and watch qualifications. Record long-term tenor, analytical perimeter, report observation date and effective action date separately. Unknown effective dates stay blank with a reason; the report can support an observation without establishing the current/effective rating. Capture the full rating row, facility association, headers and footnotes. Split a combined LT/ST cell only when the LT component is explicit; never convert a parent rating or ST grade into the issuer's LT grade.

**`cr_rating_outlook`** is an explicit agency outlook linked to its grade, facility, entity, tenor and dates. Preserve raw wording. Only explicit stable, positive, negative and developing labels can map to that vocabulary using an approved versioned mapping; otherwise leave normalization blank for review. Watch status is a qualifier, not an outlook. Management business outlook is excluded. Missing outlook never defaults to stable; explicit inapplicability needs evidence.

**`cr_liquidity_assessment`** is the agency's exact liquidity classification plus connected rationale atoms. Keep agency-specific wording; no universal score is defined. Capture classification and all material connected cash, accrual, debt-service, line-headroom, restrictions and conditional-support passages, including continuations. Distinguish realized cash from forecasts, sanctioned facilities from available cash, and group access from unrestricted entity cash. A heading alone is insufficient if material rationale is omitted. Record classification as an observation and supporting rationale as linked `support` rows under the same field; do not turn support amounts into new features.

**`cr_interest_coverage_ratio`** is explicitly disclosed interest coverage, in `x`, preserving the numerator/denominator definition. EBIT/interest and EBITDA/finance cost differ. Capture exact financial interval, actual/estimated/projected basis, agency adjustments, units and accounting perimeter with row/column headers and formula notes. DSCR is excluded. Do not recompute from unrelated numbers. Unknown formula supports an unresolved review disclosure, not a comparable accepted ratio. Conventional industrial coverage generally does not apply to banks/lenders/insurers absent a separately approved definition. An undefined denominator is not zero.

**`cr_working_capital_limit_utilization_pct`** is a disclosed numerical percentage of sanctioned working-capital limits. Preserve average, peak or point-in-time statistic, exact window, denominator limits, borrower, lender/facility aggregation and fund/nonfund basis. Preserve the literal statistic even if it cannot be normalized. Ambiguous wording such as “average maximum” requires human adjudication; do not guess average or peak. Qualitative utilization or monetary headroom does not supply an invented percentage. Do not infer a 12-month window from the report year, average different facilities, derive unrestricted cash, clip over-100 values or make an unused/negligible description numerical zero without explicit support.

## A — Coverage decisions: one row per document/field/reviewer

The coverage template contains 60 rows with only pair IDs, original IDs/hashes and field names populated. Reviewer, page references, applicability, completeness and all decisions are blank. Each reviewer uses all 60 rows; two independent copies produce 120 coverage decisions only after humans actually complete them.

- Keep `pair_id`, `candidate_id`, `audit_alias`, `original_sha256` and `feature_name` unchanged. `candidate_id` is the original DI-02 stable document ID, not an extracted-answer ID; full original hashes govern joins. Fill `coverage_record_id` as `<reviewer>-<pair_id>-v1`, with the same reviewer ID throughout. Fill submission/protocol/dictionary versions, aware UTC timestamps such as `2026-11-01T09:00:00Z`, source identity check, attestation and exposure details. A later correction gets a new immutable record ID/version and `supersedes_record_id` plus reason.
- Record company and exact rated legal entity with page-based identity evidence. Do not infer a ticker or production company ID. Record the search universe in `scope_period_search_universe`: the exact supplied PDF/hash, all included report versions, current/historical periods and facilities being reviewed. Never claim issuer-wide absence from one PDF.
- `applicability_decision` is `APPLICABLE`, `NOT_APPLICABLE` or `APPLICABILITY_UNRESOLVED`; give source/business evidence. This is a review decision, not an added dictionary value state. Unknown sector or lending structure does not establish N/A. Fields may have mixed applicable/unresolved dimensions: explain them and link separate claims.
- Write a page-by-page `page_coverage_log`, for example the invented format `physical 1: cover and rating table visually checked; physical 2: rationale and notes checked; physical 3: annexure checked`. Enumerate each physical page, even pages with no relevant text; printed page numbers are separate. One shared human-authored coverage log may be referenced by exact filename, record/section and SHA256 across five rows, but each field requires its own sign-off. Do not put a bare “all pages” or just a search term in this cell.
- Record table/annexure/footnote review, cross-page continuation links, external schedules, unreadable regions and unresolved layout gaps in their named columns. Log referenced but unavailable attachments. If they could change the answer within the defined search universe, leave coverage partial; do not silently redefine the universe to hide a gap.
- Link every observation/support row in `claim_ids`, control rows in `control_ids`, and every unresolved matter in `unresolved_issues`. At submission record reviewer sign-off, review time and `verification_decision`. A positive with an unresolved additional disclosure can have `DISCLOSED` coverage summary, linked unresolved rows and only `PARTIAL` coverage. Same-grain incompatible disclosures remain `CONFLICTING`.

### Use the dictionary's orthogonal status vocabulary

- `value_state`: `DISCLOSED`, `NOT_DISCLOSED`, `NOT_APPLICABLE`, `UNRESOLVED`, `CONFLICTING`. Explicit zero is `DISCLOSED`. Non-disclosed states have blank normalized values. Conflicting alternatives stay in separate evidence records; no chosen scalar or average.
- `process_reasons`: `NOT_SEARCHED`, `DOCUMENT_UNAVAILABLE`, `SOURCE_NOT_COVERED`, `EXTRACTION_FAILED`, `MISSING_CANDIDATE`, `INSUFFICIENT_EVIDENCE`, `PERIOD_UNRESOLVED`, `SCOPE_UNRESOLVED`, `APPLICABILITY_UNRESOLVED`, `INVALID_DENOMINATOR`, or an explicit reviewed reason. Separate multiple reasons with ` | `. No extraction occurs here; do not invent a parser failure for a human review gap.
- `coverage_status`: start with `NOT_ASSESSED`, then `PARTIAL`; use `COMPLETE_RELEVANT_SOURCE_REVIEW` only after enumerated visual page/table/annexure/footnote/continuation review, sign-off and resolution of all gaps capable of hiding the field. Positive discovery alone does not establish completeness. `NOT_DISCLOSED` requires complete relevant-source review, source identity, reviewer and time. `NOT_APPLICABLE` requires evidence and reviewer approval, not absence.
- `verification_decision`: `PENDING`, `VERIFIED_DISCLOSURE`, `NEEDS_REVIEW`, `REJECTED`. Reviewer verification is a source-support decision, not proof of two-review adjudication. For negative/N/A coverage decisions record evidence and sign-off, and use `NEEDS_REVIEW` until human comparison/approval; do not describe an absence as a verified positive disclosure.

Temporal eligibility is outside these annotation decisions. Never infer public availability from cover dates or filesystem times, and never replace unknown dates with a report date. A later historical export must separately retain dictionary temporal states and certified provenance.

## B — Atomic claims: add rows as needed

The claim CSV has a header only. Add a row for each distinct field × original version × legal entity × period/effective time × scope × facility × agency/scale/metric basis/statistic. Two facilities or two fiscal years require distinct rows when independently disclosed. Repeated mentions of the same observation link through `duplicate_of_claim_id` and retain all locations; they do not create extra gold instances. True same-grain inconsistencies retain alternatives with `contradiction_group_id`.

Assign `claim_id` as `<reviewer>-<alias>-<field>-C0001-v1`, increasing the number within that reviewer/document/field. Copy the matching `coverage_record_id`, `pair_id`, original document ID/hash and reviewer/version metadata. Use `record_kind` = `observation`, `support` or `control` (form organization only). Supporting rationale/qualifications link through `parent_claim_id` and `linked_claim_ids`; support rows are not separate target-field positives. A control identifies exact confusable evidence and `expected_control_behavior` (reject as wrong metric, abstain on ambiguity, or source-bounded absence/N/A). Controls are human-authored after discovery, never prespecified document answers.

Fill the columns in groups:

- **Identity/attribution:** company, optional verified namespace/ID, rated legal entity, source identity evidence, agency, `assertion_origin` and `statement_kind`. Use dictionary origins `issuer_reported`, `management_statement`, `rating_agency_opinion`, `analyst_question`, `analyst_interpretation`; choose the actual attribution, not a default. Statement kinds are `actual_reported`, `agency_assessment`, `guidance`, `target`, `aspiration`, `conditional_expectation`, `question`, `interpretation`. Agency opinion does not become independently verified financial truth.
- **Scope/time:** facility/instrument, tenor, rating scale, scope (`standalone`, `consolidated`, `subsidiary`, `facility`, `segment`, `project`, `plant`, `unknown`), analytical perimeter and scope evidence. Preserve report, effective and assessment dates separately, with literals and resolved ISO dates only when supported. Record period literal/kind/start/end and fiscal-calendar evidence. Period kinds follow the dictionary: `instant`, `quarter`, `half_year`, `nine_month`, `fiscal_year`, `rolling_window`, `effective_event`, `target_interval`. Keep actual/estimate/projection distinctions. Missing dates/basis stay blank with explicit reasons.
- **Value/basis:** raw exact token and, only where justified, normalized decimal string/category, mapping version, unit/currency/scale/sign/comparator. Preserve low/high bounds, inclusivity and approximation; no midpoint. Percent values are percentage points on a 0–100 scale, without silently clipping exceptional disclosures; ratio unit is `x`. Preserve numerator, denominator, literal/normalized statistic and facility limit basis. No agency mapping is created here; leave unmapped categories blank rather than inventing one. Blank nonrelevant columns are allowed; explain material unknowns.
- **Evidence:** one-based physical PDF pages; printed labels separately; section/table identifier, row label, complete column headers, exact evidence text, units/header evidence and all footnotes/continuations. Use a descriptive table identifier if the PDF has none. Quote complete supporting context across pages; a page number or copied number alone is insufficient. Record visual scan/layout issues. Native text cannot replace original visual checking of cell alignment. This manual workflow needs no generated crops or OCR.
- **Qualifications:** preserve all material conditions, restrictions, negations, approximations, refinancing assumptions and group-access limitations in their named cells and linked support rows. Keep exact wording as evidence even if also summarized. Do not delete mitigants or caveats because they complicate normalization.
- **Checks/decision:** identity, locator integrity, normalization, semantic support, attribution, period/scope and qualifications each get `PASS`, `FAIL`, `UNRESOLVED`, or `NOT_APPLICABLE` with explanation in unresolved issues. These are check outcomes, not replacements for dictionary statuses. Every relevant check must pass before a positive becomes `VERIFIED_DISCLOSURE`. Record value state/process reasons independently. Keep discovered but insufficient disclosures for review, without accepting guessed numeric truth.

## Invented examples only — never source answers

These examples concern fictional Example Components Ltd and a fictional Agency Z, and count toward no sample gate.

- A table explicitly pairs Facility A with `Z A- (provisional)` and a separate ST grade. Correct: the explicit LT grade including minus/provisional qualifier, agency, facility and supporting headers. Incorrect: company-wide `A`, or an inferred LT grade from the ST component.
- `Credit watch with negative implications` appears without an outlook clause. Correct: preserve watch context and review outlook separately without inventing a value. Incorrect: outlook = negative or stable by default.
- `Liquidity: adequate, subject to successful refinancing in June` continues with restricted cash on the next page. Correct: classification linked to both conditions and supporting pages. Incorrect: unconditional adequate, or restricted cash called available cash.
- A table explicitly defines EBITDA/finance cost and reports FY2024 actual `4.2x` and FY2025 estimated `3.8x`. Correct: two observations, each with its own column, period and basis. Incorrect: one “current 4.2” scalar, or swapping estimated and actual. If no formula can be established, preserve the disclosure as unresolved rather than inventing EBIT/interest.
- `Average fund-based limit utilization was approximately 61–66% during the six months ended 30 September 2025`. Correct: bounds 61/66, approximation, average, fund-based denominator and supported six-month interval. Incorrect: 63.5%, peak, or a guessed full-year window. `Negligible usage` remains text for review; it is not an explicit numerical zero.
- A fully checked fictional five-page PDF contains no applicable outlook disclosure. Correct: `NOT_DISCLOSED` only with all five page entries, identity, source universe and reviewer sign-off. Incorrect: “absent” after searching only page 1. An unreadable annexure capable of holding an outlook means `PARTIAL` and unresolved absence.

## C — Independent comparison and human adjudication

After both private submissions are sealed, the coordinator compares all 60 pairs and all claims, including omissions and coverage. Match by dimensions and source evidence, not reviewer claim-number equality. Record one adjudication row per comparison issue; also record agreements so every pair has a documented outcome. Use `comparison_status` = `AGREEMENT` or `DISAGREEMENT`; describe missing submissions explicitly. Categories include existence/omission, applicability, value/sign/bounds, unit/scale, period/effectiveness, entity/facility/scope, agency/attribution, metric/statistic/denominator, qualification, citation/header support, duplication/conflict and coverage. Multiple categories can use ` | `.

Use immutable `adjudication_id` such as `DI03-ADJ-0001-v1`. Fill both reviewer identities, submission filenames and full hashes, coverage record IDs and all relevant claim IDs. Where one reviewer has no claim, say so in comparison notes and link that reviewer's coverage row. Record a comparison for agreement as well; no unrecorded pair is implicitly accepted.

The human adjudicator reopens the original and records exact source evidence/location, decision, reason, identity and UTC timestamps. `human_decision` may be `ACCEPT_A`, `ACCEPT_B`, `ACCEPT_BOTH_DISTINCT`, `ACCEPT_CORRECTION`, `AGREE`, or `WITHHOLD_UNRESOLVED`. `adjudication_status` is `PENDING`, `RESOLVED` or `UNRESOLVED`. These are workflow outcomes, not new field-value statuses. Record accepted/rejected/unresolved record IDs. A new correction row in a separate adjudicator claim/coverage copy references its predecessor and reason; never overwrite A or B. Material unresolved claims stay excluded, with unresolved exclusions frozen explicitly. Resolving a label need not force a source ambiguity into a number.

If only one human is available, use **PRELIMINARY SINGLE-REVIEW — REGRESSION ONLY** on the submission/version record and all resulting summaries. Leave reviewer B blank, note no second review, and keep independence/adjudication gates unfulfilled. A second blank template is not a second review. A single-review set cannot qualify as independently adjudicated gold or unlock the two-review extraction gate.

The coordinator and adjudicator must document every gate per tested field before a separately authorized run:

2. Obtain two independent human submissions for relevant fields, complete source discovery/coverage, source-supported identity and every material scope/period/basis/qualification. Record comparison and human adjudication, including unresolved exclusions; no inferred consensus.
3. Require **at least five accepted positive document cases per tested field**, capped at one per original document for sample adequacy, regardless of claim/facility/year count. Also require **at least two accepted field-relevant negative/ambiguous controls** per tested field, documenting source evidence, expected rejection/abstention and limitations. ### Raw scoring definition to freeze — no scoring is run here

Use one-to-one strict matching of deduplicated atomic observations on original version, field, entity, facility/scope, agency/scale, period/effective time, basis/statistic and value. Exact token/precision, sign, bounds, deterministic conversion, complete material qualifications and supporting citation/header association must be correct. Rationale support atoms are required where material but do not inflate independent classification positives. Different facility/year rows remain distinct. Wrong material dimensions or qualifications make an emitted claim FP and leave its unmatched gold claim FN. Withheld ambiguous claims are not guessed positives.

- **Raw precision:** correct deduplicated emitted evaluable observations / all deduplicated emitted evaluable observations, before human correction. No emissions means undefined precision, not 100%. Distinguish nonexistent disclosure from wrong extraction of a real disclosure; retain unsupported emissions in FP accounting.
- **Raw recall:** strictly recovered accepted resolvable observations / all accepted resolvable observations in complete-reviewed pairs. Abstention on a resolvable positive is FN. Zero positives means undefined recall. Partial pairs have provisional discovery counts, excluded from primary exhaustive recall.
- **Abstentions:** explicit abstention decisions / requested reviewed document/field pairs, with reasons and positive-pair abstentions separately. Omitted claim, malformed/empty output, timeout and explicit abstention are different events. Failure is not voluntary abstention; retries/failures must be retained and cannot improve raw scores through human repair.
- **Material errors:** counts and rates per evaluable emitted observation, relevant numerical/type subset and document, for unsupported disclosure, wrong token/sign/bounds, unit/scale, period, entity/facility/scope, agency/attribution, metric/statistic/basis, lost conditions and unsupported citations. Categories may overlap. Score unsupported absence/N/A decisions separately at pair level; report positive emissions on nominated controls and incorrect control documents / reviewed control documents. Never count unreviewed pages as true negatives.

Report TP/FP/FN and denominators by field, plus document/issuer clustering, positive-document field recovery and requested-pair response completeness. Retain the first raw output and every attempt. Human additions/corrections belong only to a separately reported verified-pipeline result. Existing frozen feasibility criteria remain ≥80% raw candidate recall per field, 100% final admitted material correctness and zero unsupported control admissions; no raw precision threshold is invented here. Empty denominators or missing evidence gates cannot pass. Raw metrics must always be visible before corrected results.
